About Reel

A personal media downloader, built on Flask and yt-dlp.

Reel runs entirely on your own machine: nothing you download is sent anywhere except the site you're downloading from. It uses yt-dlp integrations — availability depends on each link and platform. Examples include YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch clips, Pinterest, and public Drive/Dropbox links, among others.

Only download media you own the rights to, or that's licensed for reuse. Respect each platform's terms of service.

Requires ffmpeg on your system PATH for merging, audio extraction, and embedding thumbnails/metadata.
